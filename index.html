The artist

Forty years. One surface at a time.

She started with hand-painted tiles in 1989 and never stopped finding new surfaces to transform.

Thibodaux-born and self-taught, Connie Harris has spent more than forty years turning walls, cabinetry, ceilings, floors, and architectural details into original works of art. A distant cousin of Edgar Degas through his New Orleans-born mother, she carries that French Impressionist lineage in the way she sees color and light.

From her studio at Chatsworth Plantation in St. Gabriel, she creates private commissions and teaches the craft to aspiring artists — sharing the layering, patience, and instinct that make a surface feel alive. "It's God-given," she says. "I just absolutely love what I'm doing."
